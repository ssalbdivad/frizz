---
title: Goals
description: A standing prompt a thread receives on its own.
---

Give a thread a **goal** and Frizz sends it as a prompt for you: every time the agent comes to rest, on a clock you set in minutes, or both. Good for "keep going until the test suite is green" without re-asking.

<Shot src="/img/goal.png" width={1600} height={1170} alt="The goal panel above a queue card's prompt box: keep going until the test suite is green, sent at every rest and every 30 minutes." />

A goal on a clock reaches the agent even mid-turn, at its next step, without cutting off what it is doing — so it can nudge a thread that never stops.

## Bounded

A goal can stop itself after a number of sends, after a span of time, or whichever comes first: "check back every 20 minutes for the next two hours" ends on its own.

## Turning it off

Switch it off from the thread's Goal control whenever you like. The agent can also say it is finished, which ends the goal.

## Goals, schedules and snoozes

| | Runs in | Fires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Goal** | the same thread | at every rest, on a clock, or both |
| **Snooze** | the same thread, once | at a time you pick |
| **[Schedule](/docs/schedules)** | a fresh thread each time | on a calendar |
