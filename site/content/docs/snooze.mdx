---
title: Snooze
description: Park a card until later, optionally with what to do when it wakes.
---

Not everything needs an answer now. Snooze a card from the alarm clock in its header, or press <kbd>s</kbd>, and it leaves the queue for the **Snoozed** band until its time comes.

<Shot src="/img/snooze.png" width={1600} height={1170} alt="The snooze menu open from the alarm clock in a queue card's header, offering 1 hour, tomorrow morning, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, and a custom time and prompt." />

Pick an hour, tomorrow morning, a few days, a week, or a date and time of your own. "Tomorrow" rolls over at 5am, so a snooze set after midnight still means the next morning.

## With a follow-up

Attach a prompt to the snooze and the thread wakes up already working on it — "check whether CI went green and fix it if not" — instead of waking up to wait for you.

## Until its sub-agents return

A card whose worker is waiting on its own sub-agents can be snoozed until all of them are back, so it returns to the queue only when there is something to read.

Waiting on something outside — a pull request, an issue, a timer — doesn't need a snooze at all: the worker [watches it](/docs/github#watchers) and the thread stays out of the queue on its own.
