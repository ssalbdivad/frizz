Open the board on a phone — over [remote access](/docs/remote-access) — and it switches to a phone layout.

<Shot src="/img/phone.png" width={1170} height={2532} alt="Frizz on a phone: the All projects queue with three questions that need you at the top, then threads from three projects, and a New thread button." className="mx-auto max-w-xs" />

- **Tabs** for the queue, snoozed and done threads, and schedules, with one line per thread.
- **Questions answer with one tap** on the card; larger ones step through a sheet.
- **Done** files the thread and opens the next one that needs you.
- **Long-press** a row for the thread's actions.
- **New thread** is a full-screen page, with model and effort in a bottom sheet.
- **Settings** is a page of its own, and can sign this device out.
